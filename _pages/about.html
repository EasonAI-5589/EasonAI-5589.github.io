About Me

I study efficient and reliable multimodal intelligence. My research focuses on preserving useful visual information with less computation, and understanding whether robotic world models faithfully follow actions.

Research Interests

  • Robotic world models: action following, evaluation, and policy learning.
  • Reliable visual reasoning: visual grounding and hallucination mitigation.
