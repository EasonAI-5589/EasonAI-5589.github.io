WorldEcho evaluates whether action-conditioned robotic world models follow expert and off-expert actions. It combines visual integrity checks with SE(3) end-effector trajectory alignment, distinguishing visually plausible action mismatches from invalid generated rollouts.

WorldSync improves action following through broader action coverage, an Action-Forcing Expert that grounds intermediate video representations in robot dynamics, and intervention-effect supervision. Experiments on RoboTwin and real-robot tasks show improvements in action following and downstream policy learning.
