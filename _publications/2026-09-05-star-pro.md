STAR-Pro is a training-free, two-stage framework for efficient large vision-language models. Its Adaptive Stage uses pivoted QR to form an over-budget candidate pool with broad visual feature coverage. Its Progressive Stage uses evolving text-to-visual attention to refine a nested set of retained tokens across decoder layers.

The evaluation spans seven LVLMs and 18 image and video benchmarks. On LLaVA-Video-7B with 64 input frames and nominal T=16, STAR-Pro reduces visual tokens by 90.5%, retains 92.7% of baseline performance, and achieves a measured 2.24× inference speedup. Timing is measured on one A800-80GB, with batch size 1 and one generated token; it excludes CPU video decoding and preprocessing.
